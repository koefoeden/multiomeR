After GEX clustering, `scDblFinder` is applied per GEM well, with `dbr.sd = 1`, to the gene-expression counts of the retained nuclei [@germain2022_scdblfinder]. These counts are CellBender-corrected where configured. scDblFinder receives annotation-derived cluster labels: the cell-type label of an assigned cluster, or otherwise its annotation status and identifier. A configured map can optionally collapse these labels. By default, nuclei called as doublets are removed, as are all nuclei of Leiden clusters whose doublet fraction exceeds 0.5.
