GEX and ATAC values are residualized against donor, scaled log RNA and ATAC depth and the class's accessibility components described below; the Pearson correlation of the residuals is reported as a descriptive summary. The hierarchical analysis fits donor fixed intercepts, depth covariates, the accessibility components and a donor-varying slope for the within-donor-centered peak value, using project-owned compiled kernels for profiled restricted maximum likelihood and Kenward–Roger inference for the average slope; `lme4` and `pbkrtest` serve only as test references. It requires within-donor peak variation in at least eight donors; with fewer, the random-slope test has too few degrees of freedom to be informative, so such classes yield diagnostics but no tests. Unassigned clusters are not analyzed. Fits failing numerical diagnostics retain their estimates but no inferential P-value. Peaks and genes that both vary along shared chromatin states correlate whether or not they interact. The accessibility components remove the leading states: the top ten principal components, or one per ten residual degrees of freedom when fewer, of the class's donor- and depth-residualized, standardized log1p CPM of all peaks accessible in at least 5% of its pseudobulks across chromosomes. Each class's model P-values are then calibrated against background pairs scored with the same model: in each branch, peaks and genes on the same chromosome more than 10 Mb apart, twice as many as the candidate pairs, whose tests reproduce those of pairs on different chromosomes. A candidate pair's empirical P-value is the share of the class's background pairs with at least as small a model P-value, and these are Benjamini–Hochberg-corrected within each annotation class over the complete eligible pair family, counting unreliable tests. A candidate link requires an estimable positive average slope and an FDR below 0.05, excluding self-promoter peaks. SuSiE fine-mapping, with up to ten single effects and 95% credible sets whose peaks correlate with a minimum absolute correlation of at least 0.5 (purity), prioritizes peaks for genes with at least one candidate link, using the same residualized values [@wang2020_susie]. These model-based associations do not establish causal enhancer–gene regulation.

Top-link plots rank estimable positive hierarchical associations by nominal P-value, without a significance cutoff, excluding self-promoter peaks; gene-body peaks remain eligible. Each plot shows the gene context, the focal class's insertion coverage and the donor-residual scatter.
