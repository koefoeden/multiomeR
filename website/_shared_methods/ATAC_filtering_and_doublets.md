The ATAC branch starts from the GEX-retained nuclei. The peak-calling groups and the consensus peak matrix are built on this cell set. Per-nucleus quality-control metrics, such as the counts in peaks and their fraction, are calculated from the matrix and evaluated against configured aggregation-level exclusions. After ATAC clustering, a separate ATAC-based `scDblFinder` analysis follows per GEM well, with `dbr.sd = 1`. Instead of scDblFinder's internal per-well feature aggregation, peaks are grouped once into 50 feature groups. The groups come from *k*-means clustering of the peaks' aggregation-wide LSI coordinates on dimensions 2–20 (loadings scaled by the singular values, as in scDblFinder). The peaks of each group are summed with BPCells before the unchanged classifier is called. This adaptation bounds memory use, but its scores are not expected to equal those obtained with the internal aggregation. ATAC doublets are removed at the nucleus and cluster level as for GEX.
