SCAVENGE-style trait-relevance scores are calculated from the nucleus-level z-scores on the WNN graph with a local sparse-matrix implementation of the SCAVENGE propagation strategy rather than the reference package [@yu2022_scavenge]. Nuclei whose one-sided normal-tail probability is at most 0.05 are seeds, keeping those with the highest z-scores when they exceed the maximum seed fraction, 5% of nuclei by default; when no nucleus qualifies, all scores are zero. The nonzero support of the WNN graph is converted to binary adjacency, degree-zero nuclei are excluded, and seed signal is propagated by a random walk with restart probability 0.05 by default until convergence; nuclei that no seed reaches remain unscored. Scores are capped at their 95th percentile, min–max scaled and multiplied by the mean z-score of the top 1% of nuclei. Unlike the reference, no permutation P-values are computed; scores are summarized per metadata grouping.
