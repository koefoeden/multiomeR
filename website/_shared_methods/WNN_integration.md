GEX and ATAC representations are combined with a BPCells-native implementation of the Seurat weighted-nearest-neighbor (WNN) strategy [@hao2021_multimodal; @parks2025_bpcells]. The GEX and ATAC embeddings, after optional Harmony correction, are aligned by barcode and L2-normalized. For each modality separately, 200 candidate neighbors are then found with hierarchical navigable small world (HNSW) search [@malkov2020_hnsw]. A native routine sets each cell's kernel width in each modality from the configured neighbor count, following Seurat's small-SNN bandwidth strategy. Per-cell modality weights are derived from these kernels by comparing within-modality with cross-modality predictions. The candidates of both modalities are then pooled, and those with the highest weighted kernel scores become the final neighbors. Seurat's weights tend to 0 or 1 as the number of nuclei grows, because the kernel widths shrink with density. The weights are therefore estimated within random subsets of at most 10,000 nuclei, whereas the neighbors are selected among all nuclei. BPCells builds an SNN graph from the joint neighbors for Leiden clustering, and UMAP uses the joint neighbor lists directly. The implementation does not call `Seurat::FindMultiModalNeighbors()`. Because HNSW replaces Seurat's Annoy search, exact equality is not expected. Its validation is instead a similarity contract on modality weights and neighbor overlap.
