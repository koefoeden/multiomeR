Annotation-class pseudobulk deviations are calculated separately rather than by averaging the nucleus-level results. ATAC counts are summed by the final WNN cell-type labels, which the other analyses also use. A betterChromVAR background model is fitted to the resulting peak-by-class matrix. The expected accessibility of each peak averages the depth-normalized class profiles with equal weight. Each class is thus compared with the average class rather than with a reference dominated by the most abundant classes. Background peaks are matched on this expectation and on GC and fragment-length bias. The raw deviation is the observed-minus-background accessibility of the weighted peaks relative to their expected accessibility. A relative deviation additionally standardizes the raw deviations across annotation classes within each trait. Analytic z-scores, rescaled for the peak weights as for nuclei, give one-sided P-values. Their Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted values across all traits and annotation classes are retained. Plot labels mark unadjusted z-scores of at least 1.645 and 2.326. When effect sizes are available, an absolute-effect branch weights variants by posterior probability times absolute effect size. When only loci carry effects, each locus's lead-variant effect is used. These weights are rescaled to sum to the retained variants' posterior probabilities. Locus-level attribution decomposes each class deviation additively into contributing loci and variants, whose contributions sum to the class totals. The loci and variants are labeled with the highest-scoring Open Targets locus-to-gene genes [@mountjoy2021_l2g]. Detail plots are drawn for classes with a positive deviation and a z-score of at least 1 by default, in either weighting. They show the top loci by absolute contribution and by combined contribution and effect-size rank.
