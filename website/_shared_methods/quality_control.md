Each GEM well supplies a Cell Ranger ARC count directory. Gene-expression counts are imported into BPCells from the filtered feature-barcode matrix, or from a CellBender output when configured [@parks2025_bpcells; @fleming2023_cellbender]. ATAC fragments are imported from the Cell Ranger fragment file. Both carry a GEM-well-prefixed barcode, so nuclei remain distinct across wells. Per-well quality-control metrics are calculated for the barcodes that Cell Ranger called as cells. BPCells calculates ATAC metrics such as TSS enrichment and nucleosome signal. Gene-expression metrics, such as the fraction of counts from mitochondrial genes (names beginning with `MT-`, ignoring case), are calculated from the imported matrix. Optional genotype demultiplexing runs cellsnp-lite on the ATAC alignments, with a minimum minor-allele frequency of 0.1 and 20 reads per SNP [@huang2021_cellsnp_lite]. Vireo then demultiplexes the barcodes with the supplied donor genotypes [@huang2019_vireo]. The most likely singlet donor replaces the GEM-well donor identifier of every barcode. Vireo's singlet, doublet and unassigned calls and probabilities are recorded as metadata for the exclusions. A BPCells-native implementation of AMULET streams the stored fragments of up to 1,000 bp [@thibodeau2021_amulet]. It skips fragments on chrM, chrX and chrY and, for GRCh38, fragments in the AMULET authors' list of repeats, segmental duplications and blacklisted regions. It reproduces the overlap metrics and q-values of `scDblFinder::amulet()` but applies no automatic filter [@germain2022_scdblfinder].

Configured GEM-well exclusions are filter expressions evaluated on the called cells of each well. Demultiplexing and AMULET results can therefore serve as exclusion criteria before aggregation-level analysis. Called cells absent from the imported gene-expression matrix are always excluded. The remaining nuclei of the selected wells are combined without further filtering.
