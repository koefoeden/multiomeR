GEX and ATAC representations are combined with a BPCells-native implementation of the Seurat weighted-nearest-neighbor (WNN) strategy [@hao2021_multimodal; @parks2025_bpcells]. The GEX and ATAC embeddings, after optional Harmony correction, are aligned by barcode and L2-normalized, and 200 candidate neighbors are found separately for each modality with hierarchical navigable small world (HNSW) search [@malkov2020_hnsw]. A native routine applies Seurat's small-SNN bandwidth strategy to set a per-cell kernel width from the configured neighbor count, per-cell modality weights are derived from within- versus cross-modality prediction kernels, and the union of candidates is ranked by the weighted kernel score to select the final neighbors. Seurat's weights tend to 0 or 1 as the number of nuclei grows, because the kernel widths shrink with density, so they are estimated within random subsets of at most 10,000 nuclei, while the neighbors are selected among all nuclei. Each nucleus is placed first in its own neighbor lists when the approximate search misses it. BPCells builds the joint SNN graph, which is clustered with Leiden and embedded with UMAP using the precomputed neighbors. The implementation does not call `Seurat::FindMultiModalNeighbors()`; because HNSW replaces Seurat's Annoy search, exact equality is not expected, and its validation is a similarity contract on modality weights and neighbor overlap.
