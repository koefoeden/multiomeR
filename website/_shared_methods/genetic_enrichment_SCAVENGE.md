SCAVENGE-style trait-relevance scores are calculated from the nucleus-level z-scores on the WNN graph. They use a local sparse-matrix implementation of the SCAVENGE propagation strategy rather than the reference package [@yu2022_scavenge]. Nuclei whose one-sided normal-tail probability is at most 0.05 are seeds. When they exceed the maximum seed fraction, 5% of nuclei by default, those with the highest z-scores are kept. When no nucleus qualifies, all scores are zero. The nonzero support of the WNN graph is converted to binary adjacency, and degree-zero nuclei are excluded. Seed signal is propagated by a random walk with restart probability 0.05 by default until convergence. Nuclei that no seed reaches remain unscored. Scores are capped at their 95th percentile, min–max scaled and multiplied by the mean z-score of the top 1% of nuclei. Unlike the reference, the pipeline computes no permutation P-values. Scores are summarized per metadata grouping.
